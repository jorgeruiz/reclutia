## 1. Hero

### hero-home.webp

- **Seccion:** Hero principal
- **Dimensiones:** 1200 x 800 px
- **Tipo:** generada
- **Tratamiento visual:** Fotografia corporativa moderna con tonalidad fria
  alineada a la paleta navy/azul. Iluminacion natural lateral, profundidad de
  campo moderada (sujeto enfocado, fondo ligeramente desenfocado). Sin filtros
  calidos ni saturacion excesiva. Composicion limpia, aire a la izquierda para
  que el texto del hero no compita con el sujeto.
- **Prompt de generacion:**
  Professional corporate photography of a confident business consultant in a
  modern office environment having a conversation with a client across a clean
  desk. Natural side lighting from large windows, shallow depth of field with
  background softly blurred. Cool-neutral color temperature, navy blue and white
  tones in the environment. The consultant is dressed in professional business
  attire, warm expression, direct eye contact with the client. Composition
  leaves negative space on the left third for text overlay. Clean, contemporary
  office with minimal decoration. Shot at eye level, 85mm equivalent focal
  length. No text, no logos, no watermarks. Photorealistic, editorial quality.
  Aspect ratio 3:2 landscape, 1200x800px.

## 3. Headhunting ejecutivo

### headhunting-ejecutivo.webp

- **Seccion:** Headhunting ejecutivo (seccion oscura con clip-path reveal)
- **Dimensiones:** 1400 x 700 px
- **Tipo:** generada
- **Tratamiento visual:** Fotografia ejecutiva de alto nivel. Tonalidad oscura,
  low-key lighting, fondo negro o navy profundo. El sujeto transmite autoridad
  y discrecion. Iluminacion tipo Rembrandt o split lighting para dramatismo
  contenido. Esta imagen se revela con clip-path sobre fondo navy, asi que debe
  tener tonalidades oscuras que se integren con el fondo #0A1E3F.
- **Prompt de generacion:**
  Executive portrait photography, low-key dramatic lighting. A senior business
  executive in a dark navy suit, sitting confidently in a high-end office or
  boardroom. Rembrandt lighting with the main light source from the upper left,
  creating defined shadows. Deep navy and dark grey background tones that blend
  with a #0A1E3F navy background. The subject projects authority, discretion,
  and experience. Shallow depth of field, the background is very dark and
  minimal. No smile, composed expression. Ultra-high-end editorial quality, like
  a Fortune 500 magazine cover. No text, no logos. Aspect ratio 2:1 landscape,
  1400x700px.
